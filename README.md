### [Depthtective](https://github.com/Luigina2001/Depthtective)

**A depth-aware framework for spatio-temporal deepfake detection**<br>
*Published in **Array** (Elsevier), vol. 32, 2026*

Depthtective detects manipulated videos using only **two adjacent frames**: the absolute residuals of the aligned RGB frames and of their **monocular depth maps** are fused into a compact four-channel tensor, classified by a standard CNN. A **contrastive learning** variant reaches an **AUC of 97.26%** on Celeb-DF and **98.35%** on DFDC.
